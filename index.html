About Me

Hi there! 👋 I am a machine learning researcher in Dr Sharib Ali's AI in Medicine and Surgery (AIMS) lab at the University of Leeds, where I completed my MSc in Advanced Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence) with Distinction. My research sits at the intersection of multimodal and vision-language models, foundation-model evaluation, interpretability and efficient language models. I am particularly interested in what models actually learn from the data and language they are trained on, and in measuring it faithfully, from medical imaging to low-resource languages. Beyond research, I am actively involved in the AI community through teaching, writing, podcasting and women-in-computing networks.

During my Master's, I published four first-author papers in 2026. FaithCLIP was presented as an oral contribution at the CaPTion Workshop, MICCAI 2026 in Strasbourg 🇫🇷. My paper Queryable Is Not Grounded was presented at the Explainable Computer Vision (eXCV) Workshop at ECCV 2026 in Malmö 🇸🇪. I also published on tokeniser coverage for Indic scripts at the GlobalSouthAI Workshop, co-located with IJCAI-ECAI 2026, and on small language models at ACM womENcourage 2026. I was supported by an ACM-W Europe Scholarship and a BCSWomen Lovelace Colloquium Full Scholarship.

Before my Master's, I spent three years at UST, as an AI Engineer. Before that, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University, Kerala, India 🇮🇳 with Distinction, and I was awarded an Advancing Inclusion Scholar at Grace Hopper Celebration India 2025.

I am always glad to collaborate and talk about new ideas, so please do reach out! 😊

      Experience

      • Graduate Researcher, AI in Medicine and Surgery (AIMS) Lab, University of Leeds 🇬🇧. I built a supervision signal derived by fixed rules from lesion geometry, so the effect of language on a medical vision-language model could be tested as a controlled intervention. I designed and trained FaithCLIP in PyTorch on a multi-GPU SLURM cluster. Retraining on inverted captions turned zero-shot recovery from 94.3 to 6.1 AUROC on unseen CVC-ClinicDB. I validated the grounding protocol on Kvasir-SEG (endoscopy) and PH2 (dermoscopy).2025 to present
      • AI Engineer, UST, Kerala, India 🇮🇳. I fine-tuned the multilingual model (six Indian languages) behind an automotive client's sentiment platform on NVIDIA NeMo, with NVIDIA and the client, on the client's on-premise infrastructure. I deployed a real-time fall-detection model to smartwatches through ONNX Runtime at 95% precision for a shipyard client. I built a production retrieval-augmented generation platform and was lead backend developer on a conversational AI product launched as SaaS. I led pilot testing and phased rollouts, and automated internal workflows, cutting manual effort on the affected processes by roughly 40%.2022 to 2025

      Projects

      • FaithCLIP: A colonoscopy vision-language model with a PVTv2-B2 encoder, a U-Net decoder and occupancy-weighted pooling, aligned to a frozen BiomedCLIP text tower. The text branch is dropped at inference, so the model runs image-only yet stays language-queryable.2026
      • FinSight: An agentic personal-finance assistant on Google ADK, Gemini and MCP, with six read-only tools, safety guardrails before and after the model, and an evaluation harness that gates CI. Live demo.
      • Small language models for low-resource multilingual NLP: TinyLlama-1.1B fine-tuned with LoRA and QLoRA on more than 130,000 Indic-language sentiment samples, using approximately 58% less GPU memory than mBERT at competitive quality. Public demo.2025 to 2026
      • Codelex: A browser extension that helps developers with dyslexia, and other neurodivergent developers, read code and documentation on GitHub. Built in one day by Team Codelex at the ACM womENcourage 2026 Hackathon.2026
